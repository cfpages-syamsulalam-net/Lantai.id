# Topical Authority — lantai.id

## Role and boundary

Lantai.id should become an Indonesian flooring decision and lifecycle reference: how occupants, owners, designers, buyers, contractors, and facility teams choose, specify, prepare for, install, inspect, maintain, diagnose, repair, and replace floor systems. It can cover materials ranging from ceramic and stone to wood, resilient flooring, carpet, resin, concrete, rubber, sports surfaces, and synthetic grass, while keeping direct product/service routes commercial.

The domain’s organizing entity is the floor assembly and the user decision—not a claim to own every material’s full chemistry or every construction trade. Epoxy.co.id, Kayu.co.id, Concrete.co.id, and other owned specialist domains may independently cover the same material from their own viewpoints. Cross-domain overlap is allowed and is not cannibalization. Within Lantai.id, however, one page must own each broad query, and product hubs, location templates, archives, and planned references must not compete for the same intent.

General structural slab design, standalone waterproofing, structural crack injection, landscaping, and whole sports-facility construction remain outside the central flooring boundary. Lantai.id may cover the interfaces that determine floor performance.

## Existing coverage and risks

- The sitemap is almost entirely generated geography coverage. It is not evidence of 9,647 distinct reader problems or local operations.
- Material and sports stems are repeated across roughly 489 Indonesian locations, creating large doorway, crawl, duplication, and reputation risk. Any retention decision needs GSC, backlink, lead, local-service, and content-substance evidence.
- Commercial hubs are often substantive but mix sales, generic education, price, and installation intent. Planned neutral pages must take one job and link to commercial hubs rather than restating them.
- Archive pagination alone accounts for 257 sitemap URLs and should not be treated as editorial coverage.
- Duplicate sitemap indexes, an unindexed `500-sitemap.xml`, generated README URL lists, root ad fragments, and directory routes increase operational ambiguity.
- General waterproofing and concrete injection are adjacent trades, not flooring taxonomy. They belong only where substrate and floor interfaces require them.
- Technical claims concerning slip, fire, acoustics, accessibility, healthcare, food facilities, ESD, sports performance, waterproofing, chemicals, or structural cracks require primary sources and expert review.
- Broad city pages should not be “improved” by adding generic city paragraphs. Local pages require an actual local operating or evidence difference.

## Related-domain opportunities

- Epoxy.co.id may independently cover epoxy chemistry, formulations, testing, application, safety, and repair. Lantai.id owns the floor-system comparison and assembly/interface viewpoint.
- Kayu.co.id may publish timber material, species, drying, preservation, structures, and furniture content. Lantai.id focuses on floor products, subfloor, installation, use, defects, and maintenance.
- Concrete.co.id may cover mix design, reinforcement, casting, curing, and testing. Lantai.id covers exposed/topped slab surfaces only where flooring performance depends on them.
- Taman.co.id may cover landscape and natural turf; Lantai.id covers synthetic surface selection, base/drainage interface, seams, heat, wear, and care.
- Specialist contractor domains can publish transactional methods and cases independently. Their content does not reserve queries away from Lantai.id.
- Cross-domain links should be transparent and useful; cross-domain similarity must never be entered in this domain’s cannibalization register.

## Internal-link architecture

- `/` links to LNT-01 selection, LNT-02 substrate readiness, the canonical material hubs, LNT-17 diagnosis, LNT-20 calculators, and LNT-21 contractor support.
- Every planned article links to its parent hub; each of the 21 hubs lists all eight children.
- Reader sequence: LNT-01 shortlist → LNT-02 substrate → chosen LNT-03–LNT-13 material → LNT-14 design interfaces → LNT-16 installation/QC → LNT-18 care. LNT-15 supplies use-case entry points.
- LNT-17 defect pages link backward to substrate/material/installation causes and forward to LNT-18 repair/renewal decisions.
- LNT-19 is a mandatory contextual link from wet, public, healthcare, school, sport, ESD, acoustic, fire, emissions, and accessibility content.
- LNT-20 calculators link to the exact material assumptions and then to LNT-21 scope/bid/handover documents.
- Commercial hubs receive links only when the reader’s informational task naturally progresses to product or service evaluation.
- Location templates must not be added to global related-link blocks; retained local pages link only where they contain real local evidence.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify product composition and terminology through manufacturer TDS/SDS; do not call SPC or vinyl “wood” merely because the pattern resembles wood.
- Verify named Indonesian and international standards at publication time through the issuing authority. Do not invent dimensions, tolerances, classifications, or acceptance values.
- Slip, fire/smoke, emissions, ESD, acoustics, accessibility, hygiene, sports performance, structural crack, and waterproofing content requires competent review and application-specific evidence.
- Prices, waste factors, productivity, service life, warranties, pack coverage, and lead times must be dated and conditional.
- Installation guidance states prerequisites, environmental limits, stop conditions, inspection points, and when trained professionals are required.
- Case studies use only real projects with authorization, baseline, system, dates, original images, QC evidence, limitations, and outcomes.
- Local pages require actual local service/evidence differences. Swapping a city, climate cliché, or generic shipping claim is insufficient.
- P0/P1 pages maintain a source log, review date, reviewer role, and update trigger.
